SnoozeBerry

The alarm clock that gets politer the longer you ignore it. Wake up. Please. No pressure.

How It Works

The first alarm is firm and normal. Each snooze makes it gentler: snooze 1 is "Good Morning!", snooze 3 is "Whenever you're ready, no rush" and by snooze 7 it's quietly apologising for bothering you.

The twist: after the 9th snooze it gives up and says "I believe in you," then plays one sad violin note.

The Snooze Escalation
